★ B.Tech Capstone

LexAssist AI — Indian Legal Semantic Retrieval System

My B.Tech capstone: a legal research assistant that reads long Indian court judgments, splits them into chunks, converts each chunk into a sentence embedding (all-MiniLM-L6-v2), and searches FAISS vector indexes to surface the most relevant statutes and precedent cases. Results are ranked by similarity score with exact page references and chunk-level analysis. For example, a 162-page CBI fraud judgment (317 chunks) maps to IPC Sections 415 and 420. A Flask web interface also lets users search statutes with plain-language queries.

  • Top-5 statute and top-5 precedent matches for every judgment
  • Chunk-by-chunk analysis with page ranges and similarity scores
  • Natural-language statute search through a Flask web UI
